% A shorthand decoration, such as H for !fermata! or T for !trill!, may be
% followed by white space before the note, rest or chord that it
% decorates, as section 4.14 of the abc standard allows (GitHub issue
% #73).  parsenote() (parseabc.c) required the note straight after the
% decoration, so that "H c" was a "Malformed note" and lost its fermata.
% Tune 2 is tune 1 without this white space: abc2midi plays both alike.
X:1
T:Shorthand decorations followed by white space
C:abc2midi test
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=120
K:C
HC .E H[CE] Hz |Td .e ~f Rg |'a .Tb uc vd |.Lc'4 |]

X:2
T:Shorthand decorations without white space
C:abc2midi test
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=120
K:C
HC .E H[CE] Hz |Td .e ~f Rg |'a .Tb uc vd |.Lc'4 |]
